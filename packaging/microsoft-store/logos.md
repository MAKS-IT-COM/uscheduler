# Microsoft Store logos

Upload the PNG files in [logos/](logos/) on Partner Center → Store listings → **Store logos**. They use the same mark as `src/MaksIT.UScheduler.UI/Assets/icon.png`.

Regenerate with `packaging/microsoft-store/New-StoreLogos.ps1` (Windows PowerShell 5.1).

PNG only. Poster and box art must be under 50 MB. App tiles must be under 5 MB.
